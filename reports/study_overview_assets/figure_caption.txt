Study overview of waveform self-supervision and representation evaluation. A predictable switching oscillator provides known generative factors, whereas resting-state EEG (ds004504) provides observed physiology without corresponding ground-truth latent factors. The synthetic panel shows an actual held-out trajectory: one noisy observation channel, the true accumulator u, and the binary regime q (light/dark bands); dashed lines mark regime switches. The EEG panel shows two seconds of Fp1, F3, C3 and P3 from a cached study window, with constant vertical offsets and a common physical voltage scale. Causal forecasting accesses past/current context to predict future waveform patches. Bidirectional masked reconstruction uses visible context on both sides of hidden patches. Patch counts and missingness are schematic; question marks indicate unavailable target values, not model predictions. Both objectives use a Transformer encoder and waveform head. Frozen patch embeddings support linear probes of synthetic factor recovery and EEG spectral accessibility, and participant-level dementia classification (AD, FTD and healthy controls). Waveform prediction error is assessed through the waveform head against simple baselines, separately from frozen-feature probes. Untrained encoders and handcrafted features provide representation controls. BA denotes balanced accuracy; R² measures continuous-target recovery. The diagram summarizes experimental roles rather than implying identical information access, targets or difficulty across objectives and datasets.
